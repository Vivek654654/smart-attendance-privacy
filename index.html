Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Smart Attendance Platform Y ("we", "our", or "us") is developed and maintained by Vivek Pratap Narayan. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application for attendance tracking at GITA.

1. Information We Collect

To record and verify college attendance accurately, our app collects the following information:

  • User Identification: Student/Faculty Name and Roll Number / ID.
  • Camera & Photo Access: The app takes one photo during attendance submission to verify the identity of the user.
  • Location Data (GPS): Precise location data is captured at the exact moment of marking attendance to verify physical presence on the college campus.
  • Date and Time: Automatic timestamp (date and time) recorded during attendance logging.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is strictly used for:

  • Verifying student identity via image and location-based geofencing during attendance submission.
  • Generating accurate date-wise and time-stamped attendance records for GITA administration.
  • Preventing fraudulent or proxy attendance entries.

3. Data Protection and Sharing

We value your privacy and security. The collected photos, location coordinates, and attendance data are stored securely and used solely for academic tracking within GITA. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with any third-party advertisers.

4. Permissions Required

  • Camera Permission: Required to capture a verification photo during attendance submission.
  • Location Permission (Fine/Coarse Location): Required to ensure the user is present within the authorized campus premises when marking attendance.
